# Minigame Rules

Minigames may be added only when they support the grow simulation fantasy.

A minigame should:

* connect to plant care, learning, Buddy, or progression
* be short and mobile-friendly
* avoid punishing casual players too harshly
* reward skill or attention without breaking the main simulation
* use existing economy and progression patterns

Avoid:

* disconnected arcade mechanics
* excessive grind
* rewards that overpower normal care
* separate currencies without strong reason
* large systems hidden inside a small feature approval

Every minigame concept must define entry point, duration, reward limits, failure behavior, replay rules, and affected systems.
