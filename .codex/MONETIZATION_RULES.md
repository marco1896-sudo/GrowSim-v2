# Monetization Rules

Monetization must support the premium grow simulation fantasy.

Do not add monetization hooks, paid advantages, ads, subscriptions, or store logic without explicit concept approval.

Allowed directions:

* cosmetics
* convenience that does not break simulation
* optional premium polish
* fair temporary boosts with clear limits
* supporter-style offers

Avoid:

* pay-to-win pressure
* intrusive ads
* manipulative timers
* rewards that make plant care irrelevant
* hidden economy changes

Any monetization concept must explain player value, economy impact, privacy impact, and rollback risk.
