# Quality Checklist

Each asset must pass these checks before production promotion.

- Transparent background for plant, leaf, and overlay assets.
- No pot.
- No soil.
- Full plant visible for full-plant assets.
- No cropped leaves or buds.
- Consistent centerline.
- Consistent bottom anchor.
- Symptoms match metadata.
- No extra objects.
- Readable at 320 px height.
- Readable at 180 px height.
- Not too photorealistic or noisy.
- Not too cartoonish.
- No text unless diagram asset.
- No impossible symptom combination unless pH_lockout or mixed diagnosis.
- No duplicated filenames.
- Metadata exists.
- File path matches `ASSET_INDEX.json`.
- Filename uses lowercase snake_case.
- Severity is visually proportional to metadata.
- Compatible growth stage is realistic.
