## nitrogen_deficiency
- Affected leaf age: older/lower leaves
- Visual pattern: older leaves yellow first, general chlorosis progressing upward
- Common confusion: magnesium deficiency or natural late flower fade
- Diagnostic hint: Older leaves fade first; check feed strength and stage before diagnosing.
