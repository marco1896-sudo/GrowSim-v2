# Pest Prompts

Each pest has three asset types: full-plant damage, leaf-detail damage, and pest-card identification.

## spider_mites
- Full-plant damage: fine stippling, dull leaves, webbing only in heavier infestation
- Pest card concept: tiny mites and fine web strands on leaf underside
- Leaf detail: stippling, tiny pale dots, possible webbing
- Severities: light, medium, heavy
- Gameplay event wording: spider mites pressure is affecting the plant.
- Diagnosis hints: inspect leaf undersides, compare damage pattern, check environment and recent events.
