# Overlay Prompts

Transparent overlays should not include full plants. They are runtime layers that can sit on top of approved plant assets.

## powdery_mildew_overlay_light_v001.png
- Target folder: output/overlays/
- Severity: light
- Metadata hints: condition_type=overlay; transparent_background=true
- Prompt:
```text
Transparent PNG overlay only, no full plant, no background, no text. Create subtle transparent white powder patches suitable for compositing over cannabis plant assets in a premium mobile game. Keep it subtle and readable. Avoid: pot, soil, ground plane, grow tent, lamp, tools, hands, people, labels, UI, watermarks, background scenery, cropped leaves, cropped buds, impossible cannabis morphology, random flowers, cartoon exaggeration, noisy photorealistic clutter, text inside the image, duplicate plants, malformed leaves, severe symptoms that contradict requested severity.
```
- QA notes: must not contain a complete plant, pot, soil, or UI element.
