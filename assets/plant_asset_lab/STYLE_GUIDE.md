# Plant Asset Style Guide

## Visual Direction

Assets should feel premium, clean, game-like, and biologically plausible. The style target is realistic cannabis anatomy with slight stylization for readability, not noisy photo cutouts and not cartoon icons.

## Plant Style

- Cannabis plant only.
- No pot, soil, background, tools, lamps, hands, grow tent, or environmental props.
- Full plant visible, front-facing, centered, with a consistent virtual bottom anchor.
- Main plant canvas: 2048 x 2048 transparent PNG.
- Leaf detail canvas: 1536 x 1536 transparent PNG.
- Strong silhouette at mobile sizes.

## Symptom Style

Symptoms must be visible but restrained. Light symptoms should be subtle, medium symptoms should be clear, and heavy symptoms should be severe without becoming biologically absurd. Avoid impossible combinations unless the asset is explicitly pH lockout or mixed diagnosis.

## Diagram Style

Diagrams are premium mobile educational cards with clean dark or neutral backgrounds, minimal labels, icon-like callouts, and clear hierarchy. Avoid paragraphs and avoid claims that gameplay systems cannot support.

## Mobile Readability

Every asset must read at 320 px height and remain identifiable at 180 px height. Prefer broad silhouette clarity, clean value separation, and symptom placement that survives downscaling.

## Biological Plausibility

- Old-leaf deficiencies should appear on lower/older leaves first.
- New-growth issues should affect young/top growth first.
- Pest damage should match pest behavior.
- Diseases should respect growth stage and climate risk.
- Bud-specific diseases should not appear on seedlings or veg plants.

## Do

- Keep transparent padding around leaves and buds.
- Preserve stage scale across the base growth lineup.
- Generate symptoms from a stable healthy baseline when possible.
- Show cause-specific patterns, not generic yellow damage.

## Don't

- Add pots, soil, labels, human hands, or grow equipment.
- Crop plant tips, leaves, or buds.
- Make all deficiencies look like the same yellow plant.
- Use extreme disease rendering.
- Put UI elements or text into plant and leaf assets.
