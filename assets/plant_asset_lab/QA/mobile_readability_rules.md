# Mobile Readability Rules

Test each asset at these display sizes:

- 320 px height for normal gameplay cards.
- 180 px height for compact diagnosis and event UI.
- 96 px square crop for thumbnail feasibility when relevant.

At each size, verify:

- Growth stage remains recognizable.
- Major symptom category remains recognizable.
- Silhouette is not muddy.
- Plant does not become noisy or overly photographic.
- Important damage is not hidden in tiny high-frequency detail.
- Transparent padding does not make the plant too small.
