# Visual Requirements

- Cannabis plant only.
- Premium app/game asset look.
- Realistic but slightly stylized.
- Strong silhouette readability.
- Clean lighting and transparent padding.
- No watermarks, text, UI, people, tools, pots, soil, lamps, tents, or scenery.
- Symptoms visible at phone scale without cartoon exaggeration.
- Diagram assets may contain minimal labels, but plant and leaf assets must not contain text.
