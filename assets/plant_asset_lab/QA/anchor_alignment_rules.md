# Anchor Alignment Rules

Every full-plant PNG uses a consistent virtual anchor.

- Canvas: 2048 x 2048.
- Plant centerline aligns to canvas center X.
- Bottom of visible main stem or root-stub area aligns to the shared bottom anchor Y.
- No pot, ground, or soil may define the anchor.
- Leave transparent padding around outer leaves and buds.
- Keep growth-stage scale believable: seedling small, veg expanding, flower full, harvest dense.
- Do not crop leaves, buds, cola tips, or lower stem.

QA should compare each generated full-plant asset against the approved healthy stage lineup.
